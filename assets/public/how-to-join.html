Three short steps

How to join ICE

Create your account in about a minute. No email or password, and no wallet required to start.

Create your account

Works on phone and computer

1

Open ICE

On the home page, choose Create your account or Sign in. Both start the same secure sign-in flow.

2

Complete Internet Identity

Follow the prompts on your device — a passkey, Face ID, fingerprint, or security key. It usually takes about a minute. No wallet is required to start.

3

Return and pick a username

When you come back to ICE, choose a username if asked. Then you can post. A personal site is optional — 10 ICP at mint.

Good to know

You recover access through Internet Identity on your devices — not email or a site password. Keep at least one registered device or passkey so you can sign in later.

Browsing the public feed is free. A username is free. Minting a personal site is a separate 10 ICP step (2.7 ICP canister cycles / 7.3 ICP network ops).
