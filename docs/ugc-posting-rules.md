# UGC posting rules (ICE Network)

Short operator rules for user-generated content on frostedblocks.com / ICE Network.

## Likeness and celebrities

- Do **not** use a real celebrity’s name, face, or brand likeness without rights
- Invented characters or demo names are OK only if clearly labeled **SATIRE** or **DEMO**
- Default avatars / placeholders must **not** use third-party faces scraped from the web

## Public profiles

- Do **not** put email addresses or phone numbers on **public** profiles
- Prefer Internet Identity principal / username for identity; keep contact off the public card

## Illegal and infringing content

- Ban illegal content and clear IP infringement (see Terms)
- Report tooling may exist in-product; operator may remove content and terminate access per Terms

## Store / product photos

- Product photos must be rights-cleared by the seller
- Photos are stored on the seller’s site canister and shown publicly when attached to ads
