**Audience:** Walter Wood / Frosted Blocks ops only. Not marketing.

- Operator today: **Walter Wood**, Delaware **sole proprietor**
- “Frosted Blocks” / “ICE” / “ICE Network” are **trade names only**

## Form a Delaware LLC before

1. **Stripe Connect payouts** relied on for seller volume (personal-site Store)
2. **Paid ads at scale** (acquisition spend beyond trivial tests)
3. Any **reintroduction of transferable value units** (in-app tokens, packs, credits that move between users) — these are currently **removed** and must stay removed unless counsel reviews

## Why

Sole-prop is fine for early ICE Network operation. Separating risk and clarifying contracting parties matters once money movement, ads, or transferable balances become material.
